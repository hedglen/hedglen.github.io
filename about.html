I’m interested in systems, writing, and the way people make decisions when the stakes are real. A lot of what I build or write circles those same questions from different angles.

My background includes military leadership, graduate study in business, and ongoing work in creative writing. That mix has shaped how I think about structure, pressure, communication, and the gap between theory and practice.

This site is a place to gather projects, writing, and ideas that feel worth developing in public. Some of it is polished. Some of it is still taking shape. That feels fine to me.

What I’m Interested In

Systems

Workflows, tools, structure, and practical ways to make life and work cleaner, simpler, and more intentional.

Writing

Long-form work, creative practice, revision, and the kind of writing that helps clarify what matters.

Projects

Experiments, builds, and ongoing work that sit somewhere between usefulness, curiosity, and obsession.

Why This Site Exists

I wanted a space that was mine. Not a feed, not a profile, not something rented from a platform that can change shape whenever it wants. Just a clean place to build things, write things down, and keep going.

Still building, which is probably the point.
