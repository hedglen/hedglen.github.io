About

I’m interested in systems, writing, and the way people make decisions under pressure.

My background includes military leadership, graduate study in business, and an MFA in Creative Writing. That mix shapes how I think about structure, communication, responsibility, and the tradeoffs underneath most real-world decisions.

I’m drawn to work that is practical, clear, and grounded. That applies to writing, to systems, and to the tools people rely on every day. I like clean solutions, durable frameworks, and ideas that hold up outside the room they were first discussed in.

This site is a working space. It’s where I collect projects, writing, and pieces of thought that are still taking shape. Some of it will stay rough for a while. That’s part of the point.
