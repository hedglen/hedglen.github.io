Adrift

Stigmas We Face

Rob Hedglen  ·  Poetry  ·  November 2026

Adrift.

Murky, lapping, laughing mass.
Waters circle, push and pull.
My eyes descend, my soul in tow,
To the hungry pit below.

Fight.
Breathe.

Battered and torn, I’m ripped up,
Held through the torrential torment.
From the depths where shadows sup.
Pulled high by strangers passing by.

I climb aboard and man my station.
The waters rage. The heavens roar.
We face the waves, despair no more.

Drift on as one.
