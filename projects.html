Systems and Builds

Tools, automation, and practical builds that grew out of real use.

Most of what I build starts as a practical problem — something that doesn't work the way it should, or doesn't exist yet, or only half-exists and someone decided that was good enough. I tend to disagree with that last part.

dotfiles

One-command Windows bootstrap. Drop a single PowerShell line on a fresh machine and it handles app installation, terminal config, fonts, shell profile, editor settings, and all automation. The goal is a fully productive machine in under an hour, no manual steps.

Media

mpv-config

A heavily tuned media player configuration built for a 3440×1440 ultrawide with an RTX 3070 Ti. Includes automatic HDR switching, AI-based upscaling via FSRCNNX and Anime4K shaders, hardware decoding, and a complete keyboard and mouse control scheme. The kind of project that was supposed to take an afternoon.

ytdl

A yt-dlp wrapper with quality presets, audio-only mode, and browser cookie handling that actually works. Downloads video and audio from YouTube, Twitch, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, and dozens of other platforms directly into a structured media library. Aliases are wired into the shell so it's always one command away.

media-organizer

A command-line tool that watches a download inbox and moves files into a structured library — auto-classifying content as Movies, TV Shows, or other categories based on filename parsing. Run orgmed to preview, orgmed --apply to commit. No more manually sorting downloads.

foobar2000 Theme

A portable foobar2000 setup with a custom Astra-inspired theme. Real-time spectrum analyzer, waveform seekbar, large album art with ambient effects, parametric EQ, and a dark color scheme that makes a music player feel like it was actually designed by someone who cares.
